Gantz's Resignation: A Blow to Israel's War Cabinet

Gantz's resignation marks a major blow to Israel's War Cabinet, which has been overseeing the ongoing conflict in Gaza. As the only centrist member of the cabinet, Gantz's departure leaves Netanyahu's government heavily reliant on far-right partners. Experts speculate that Netanyahu's unwillingness to approve a post-war plan for Gaza, as demanded by Gantz, may have been a key factor in his resignation.

Reasons Behind Gantz's Decision

Reasons Behind Gantz's Decision Amid growing disagreement with Prime Minister Netanyahu's handling of the Gaza offensive, Israeli War Cabinet Minister Benny Gantz declared his resignation, expressing great disappointment and displeasure with the current government's actions. Gantz demanded the establishment of a post-war plan for Gaza and prioritized the safe return of hostages. However, Netanyahu refused to acknowledge this demand, prompting Gantz's decision to leave the emergency cabinet.
