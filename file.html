What's the Scam All About?

Beware, a cunning scam is sweeping across WhatsApp, targeting unsuspecting users with deceptive calls from an international number beginning with +92. These fraudulent calls often impersonate official organizations or trusted individuals, luring victims with promises of financial gains or enticing offers. Once the connection is established, scammers attempt to extract sensitive information, such as personal details, financial data, or one-time passwords (OTPs).

How Do the Scammers Operate?

These scammers pretend to be representatives from WhatsApp support or other legitimate organizations. They often use fake names, display pictures, and claim to be able to provide technical assistance or resolve account issues. Unwary individuals who fall for their tactics may end up providing sensitive personal or financial information, which can lead to identity theft or financial loss.

What Are the Signs of a Scam Call?

Stay vigilant! Fraudsters are out there, waiting to trick you with deceptive phone calls. If you receive a WhatsApp call from an international number, especially +92, don't ignore these red flags: The caller often pretends to be a friend, family member, or a customer service representative of a known company. They may offer you an irresistible deal, ask for personal information, or request money in exchange for a promised service. Remember, if something sounds too good to be true, it probably is. Trust your instincts and report any suspicious calls.

What to Do If You Receive a Scam Call

If you've fallen prey to a scam call from +92, don't panic. The Department of Telecommunications (DoT) advises taking these immediate steps: Firstly, disconnect the call immediately to prevent further interaction with the scammer. Avoid sharing personal information, bank details, or OTPs over the phone. Next, report the incident to the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal or call DoT's toll-free helpline at 1969. Provide details of the call and the scammer's number. Additionally, consider blocking the scam number on your phone and reporting it to your telecom service provider. This helps prevent the scammer from contacting you again and flags the number for investigation.

Additional Tips to Protect Yourself

Never share personal or financial information over the phone, even if the caller claims to be from a legitimate organization. If you receive a suspicious call, hang up immediately and report the number to the DoT's helpline. Be cautious of callers who use aggressive or threatening language to pressure you into taking action. Install caller ID apps on your devices to identify incoming callers and block spam calls.

If you end up falling prey to these deceptive calls, the consequences can be severe. They can also lure you into downloading malicious software or fraudulent websites, putting your device and personal data at risk. Moreover, they may attempt to extort money from you by threatening to harm your reputation or share embarrassing information.
